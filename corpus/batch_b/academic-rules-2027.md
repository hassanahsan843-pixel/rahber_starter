# FAST-NUCES Academic Rules 2027

## 1. Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** in every course to be eligible
for the final examination. This replaces the 80% threshold from earlier versions.

The 75% threshold applies to all lecture, lab and tutorial sessions collectively.
Students whose attendance falls below 75% will be barred from the final examination
and awarded a grade of F.

## 2. Academic Warning

A student is placed on Academic Warning when their CGPA falls below **1.75** at the
end of any regular semester.

A student on Academic Warning for two consecutive semesters will be dropped from
the program.

## 3. Repeating a Course

Students may repeat a maximum of three courses. When a course is repeated, only the
most recent grade is used in GPA calculations. Both attempts appear on the transcript.

## 4. Probation and Dropping

A student will be dropped from the program if they remain on Academic Warning for
two consecutive semesters or if their CGPA does not reach 2.00 by the end of the
fourth semester.

## 5. Degree Requirements

The minimum number of credit hours required for BS degree completion remains 130.
Students must complete all core requirements, electives and project/thesis credits
specified in their program plan.
