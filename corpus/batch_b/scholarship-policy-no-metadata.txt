Content: FAST-NUCES Scholarship Policy

Merit scholarships are awarded to students who achieve a CGPA of 3.5 or above.
The scholarship covers 50% of tuition fees for one semester and is renewed if
the student maintains the qualifying CGPA. Need-based aid is available through
the financial assistance office. Applications must be submitted before the end
of the first week of each semester. Supporting documents including fee challans,
income certificates and academic transcripts are required. Students receiving
scholarships must not have any disciplinary actions on their record.
