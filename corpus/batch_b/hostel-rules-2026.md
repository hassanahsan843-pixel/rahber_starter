# FAST-NUCES Hostel Rules 2026

## 1. Curfew

All residents must return to the hostel by 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends.

## 2. Guests

No overnight guests are permitted without prior written approval from the hostel warden.

## 3. Room Inspections

Rooms are subject to weekly inspections. Students must maintain cleanliness and order.

## 4. Disciplinary Action

Violations of hostel rules may result in a warning, fine, or expulsion from the hostel.

## 5. Mess Timings

Breakfast: 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM
Lunch: 12:30 PM – 2:00 PM
Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM
