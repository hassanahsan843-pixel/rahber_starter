FAST-NUCES Library Policy 2026

Borrowing Limit: Students may borrow up to 5 books at a time.
Loan Period: 14 days with one renewal permitted.
Fine: Rs. 10 per day per book for overdue returns.
Reference books are not available for borrowing.

Access Hours: Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM; Saturday 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Digital Resources: Students have access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library and Springer
through the university network and VPN.

Silence must be maintained in all reading areas. Mobile phones must be on silent mode.
