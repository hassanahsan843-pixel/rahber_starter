## 1. Network Usage

Students are permitted to use the university network for academic purposes only.
Downloading copyrighted material, accessing blocked websites, or using VPNs for
circumventing university content filters is prohibited.

## 2. Device Registration

All personally owned devices must be registered with the IT department before
connecting to the university network. Registration requires the student ID and
device MAC address.

## 3. Email

Students are assigned a university email account upon enrollment. This account
must be used for all official communications with faculty and administration.
Personal email accounts should not be used for submitting assignments or
academic documents.

## 4. Data Security

Students must not share their university credentials with any third party.
Accounts found to be shared will be suspended immediately and disciplinary
action will be taken.

## 5. Software

Only licensed software may be installed on university computers. Students who
require specific software for academic work should submit a request to the IT
department.
