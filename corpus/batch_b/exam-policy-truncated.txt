# Examination Policy 2026

## 1. Eligibility

Students must fulfill the minimum attendance requirement and have no outstanding
dues to be eligible to sit examinations.

## 2. Conduct

Mobile phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited in examination halls.
Students found with prohibited items will face immediate disqualification.

C+ (75-79), C (70-74), D (60-69), F (below 60).
