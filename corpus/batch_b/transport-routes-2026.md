# Transport Services 2026

## 1. Available Routes

The university operates bus services on the following routes:
- Route A: Rawalpindi (Saddar) → FAST-NUCES (morning and evening)
- Route B: Islamabad (Blue Area) → FAST-NUCES (morning and evening)
- Route C: Islamabad (G-9) → FAST-NUCES (morning and evening)
- Route D: Rawalpindi (Liaquat Bagh) → FAST-NUCES (morning and evening)

## 2. Fee

Monthly transport fee: Rs. 2,500 per route.
Semester transport pass: Rs. 12,000.

## 3. Registration

Students must register for transport services before the start of each semester.
Walk-in registrations are not accepted. Changes to routes require a 30-day notice.

## 4. Conduct on Bus

Students must behave respectfully on university transport. Damage to vehicles
will be charged to the responsible student.

## 5. Timings

Morning departure: 7:30 AM from designated stops.
Evening departure: 5:30 PM from campus main gate.
