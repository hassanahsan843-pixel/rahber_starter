# FAST-NUCES Academic Rules 2026

## 1. Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum of **80%** attendance in every course.
Students who fall below 80% are barred from the final examination.

## 2. Academic Warning

A student is placed on Academic Warning when their CGPA falls below **2.00**
at the end of any regular semester.

## 3. Repeating a Course

When a course is repeated, only the most recent grade is counted.

## 4. Degree Requirements

Minimum 130 credit hours for BS degree completion.
